Problem Statement:

The User and Account Management System is designed to handle customers, employees, and account operations
across multiple banks and branches. The system aims to manage the lifecycle of user accounts, enforce
role-based access control, and support multiple currencies and account types.

The followings are the problem statements:
- Banks to have multiple branches.
- Users (customers) to hold multiple accounts across different banks and branches.
- Accounts to be of various types (Savings, Current, Term Deposit).
- Accounts to support multiple currencies (including NRI and foreign accounts).
- Users to delegate account operation rights with limited permissions (Operate Account – Limited).
- Power of Attorney (POA) and guardianship for minors to manage accounts.
- Users will have unique username and password.
- Control and power for roles to the employees and users.
- Core account operations: deposit, withdrawal (limited), check balance, and closed account (limited).
