Assumptions:

1. A user (customer) can have multiple accounts across different banks and branches.
2. Each bank can have multiple branches, and each branch belongs to one bank.
3. Accounts can be of the following types:
   - Savings Account
   - Current Account
   - Term Deposit Account (fixed term, limited operations)
4. Term Deposits do not allow direct withdrawals; maturity withdrawal only.
5. Accounts can be operated by:
   - The account holder (primary user)
   - A guardian (for minors)
   - A Power of Attorney (POA) holder
   - A delegated operator (Operate Account – Limited)
6. “Operate Account – Limited” means a user has restricted access to another user’s account.
   - Access levels: ViewOnly, DepositOnly, DepositWithdrawLimited
7. Users can perform transactions like:
   - Deposit
   - Withdrawal (subject to limits)
   - Balance Check
   - Account Closure (subject to limits)
8. All accounts are associated with a branch, account type, and currency.
9. Employees are associated with specific branches and assigned system roles.
10. System access for both employees and users.
11. Every entity includes CreatedDate/ModifiedDate fields for tracking.
